2. [P1 / R2] Current-product VAT estimates do not reach period status and record labels. The fixture has recorded net cost 3,000 cents, product VAT 10%, and sales VAT 20%. performanceReport correctly returns cost_gross_cents=3300, cost_gross_basis=current_product_vat and cost_vat_estimated=true; financials also marks cost estimated. But the selected period returns status=complete and records.profit.estimated=false, and “En çok cebine kalan” has no estimate label. tahmini omits cost_vat_estimated; the product/sales ranking aggregation has the same omission. Propagate VAT-estimate provenance to these consumers. Keep a separate VAT-estimate reason: merely increasing p.estimated would feed the existing “belgeler eşleşince kesinleşir” explanation, which is false for unrecorded historical purchase VAT. Also exclude VAT-estimated rows from “yalnız stopaj tahmini”. The main KPI, lifetime totals and cost note already label KDV estimates correctly.
